_My mother and my grandmother taught me how to use a sofrito as the base of sauces, stocks, stews, braises, etc. I soon learned that many, many cultures use the same technique with different aromatics as a base in their cooking as well. Essentially, dice aromatics and cook in oil until just translucent and fragrant. _

# Ingredients

Mirepoix (French) - 2 onion : 1 carrot : 1 celery
"Holy Trinity" (Cajun) - 2 onion : 1 bell pepper : 1 celery
Sofrito (Cuban) - 2 onion : 1 bell pepper : 1 garlic (: 1 tomato paste)

_Essential_
- [ ] oil (neutral or appropriate flavor)
- [ ] allium (onion, shallot, green onion, scallion, etc.)
- [ ] aromatic (garlic, ginger, bell pepper, lemongrass, etc.)
- [ ] salt (to taste)

_Recommended_
- [ ] extra flavor veg (carrots, celery, fennel, etc.)

_Optional_
- [ ] [[flavor paste]] (tomato paste, curry paste, etc.)
- [ ] spices

## Equipment
- knife and cutting board
- heavy pot and stovetop

# Instructions
1. Dice all vegetables.
2. In a heavy pot over medium heat, bring oil to a shimmer.
3. Add diced veggies one at a time, cooking each until fragrant. Add salt with each addition and taste, to ensure even and consistent seasoning. Allow sofrito to come back to temperature between additions.
4. (If using flavor pastes) Once veg is softened, translucent, and fragrant, clear a spot in the pan, add some fresh oil, and fry the flavor paste until fragrant. NOTE: be careful not to burn the paste!
5. Add any spices and adjust seasoning as needed.
