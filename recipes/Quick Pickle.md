_Want to pickle some vegetables? Make a brine, let em chill, and enjoy! Extends shelf life. Less healthy than a proper lactofermentation, but a lot quicker._

# Overview
1 part vegetable : 1 part brine (weight)

Brine (much easier in imperial): 1 c vinegar : 1 c water : 1 tbsp sugar : 1 tbsp salt
Brine (unitless): 1 water: 1 vinegar (>= 5% v/v acidity) + 3% w/v salt + (1-3)% w/v sugar
# Ingredients
*Per 100 g vegetables*
_Essential_
- [ ] vegetables to pickle (100 g)
- [ ] water (50 mL)
- [ ] vinegar (50 mL)
- [ ] salt (3 g)
- [ ] sugar (1-3 g)

_Recommended_
- [ ] aromatics (garlic, black peppercorns, coriander, dill, etc.)

## Equipment
- stove and saucepan
- mason jar

# Instructions
1. Prep vegetables (to taste).
2. In a saucepan, combine water, vinegar, salt, and sugar. Heat and stir until all components are dissolved.
3. Layer aromatics and vegetables in jar.
4. Pour hot brine over vegetables until just covered. Let cool.
5. Lid and refrigerate.
