# Ingredients

_Essential_
- [ ] chicken thighs, bone in (6x)
- [ ] chicken drumsticks, bone in (6x)
- [ ] chicken wings (6x)
- [ ] frying oil (peanut or vegetable) (2 L)

_Marinade_
- [ ] full fat greek yogurt (900 g)
- [ ] Crystal hot sauce (175 mL / one small bottle)
- [ ] garlic powder
- [ ] salt 

_Dredge_
- [ ] all purpose flour (2 c / 240 g)
- [ ] cornstarch (1 c / 120 g)
- [ ] smoked paprika (1 tbsp / 8 g)
- [ ] cayenne pepper (1.5 tsp / 4 g)
- [ ] white pepper (1 tsp / 3 g)
- [ ] black pepper (1.5 tsp / 4 g)
- [ ] garlic powder (1.5 tsp / 4 g)
- [ ] salt (1.5 tsp, 8 g)

# Equipment
- 2x Large bowl
- Dutch oven and stovetop
- oven and wire rack

# Instructions
1. Marinade chicken overnight.
	1. In a large bowl, combine buttermilk, hot sauce, garlic powder, and salt. Taste and adjust seasoning.
	2. Mix in chicken and coat generously.
	3. Cover and allow to marinate overnight in refrigerator.
	4. Pull chicken out and allow to come to room temperature at least half an hour before cooking.
2. Make dredge. In another large bowl, combine flour, cornstarch, and seasoning.
3. Dredge eace piece of chicken one at a time.
	1. Lift chicken and allow excess marinade to drip off.
	2. Press each piece firmly into dredge to coat all sides.
	3. Rest on wire rack for 5 minutes to allow dredge to hydrate.
	4. Repeat dredge by coating with marinade and dry dredge again. Allow to rest again.
4. Meanwhile, heat frying oil to 160C (325F) in a Dutch Oven. Make sure to include enough oil such that each piece is half submerged.
5. Fry in batches (3-4 pieces at a time) for approximately 15 minutes, turning every few minutes. Pull drumsticks first.
6. Transfer finished pieces to wire rack to dry and crisp up. Optionally, keep warm in an oven (90C / 200F)
