# Fresh Chili Pepper Reference

## Heat Scale

Perceived capsaicin concentration is measured in Scoville Heat Units (SHU). The conversion is approximately **1 SHU ≈ 1/15 mg capsaicin per kg fresh pepper**. Heat perception is roughly logarithmic, so the log₁₀ column is the most useful for intuitive comparison.

**Reading the log scale:** each +1.0 = 10x hotter. Each +0.5 ≈ 3x hotter. Each -0.5 ≈ 1/3 as hot.

|Pepper|SHU (typical midpoint)|Capsaicin (mg/g fresh)|Log₁₀ (jalapeño = 0)|Perceived Heat|Flavor Notes|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|Bell pepper|0|0|-∞|None|Sweet, vegetal — bulk and body|
|Shishito|200|0.01|-1.4|Occasional tingle|Grassy, mild — 1 in 10 is spicy|
|Banana / Pepperoncini|250|0.02|-1.3|Barely perceptible|Tangy, slightly sweet|
|Poblano|1,500|0.10|-0.5|Mild warmth|Earthy, rich — great roasted|
|Anaheim|1,500|0.10|-0.5|Mild warmth|Clean, mild — good for bulk|
|**Jalapeño**|**5,000**|**0.33**|**0.0**|**Medium**|**Bright, grassy, sharp**|
|Fresno|6,000|0.40|+0.1|Medium|Fruitier/sweeter than jalapeño|

## Substitution by Weight

To substitute one pepper for another, calculate: **target weight = jalapeño-equivalent weight ÷ 10^(log value)**.

E.g., if a recipe calls for 150 g jalapeño-equivalent:

- Serrano (log +0.5): 150 ÷ 10^0.5 ≈ **50 g**
- Thai bird's eye (log +1.2): 150 ÷ 10^1.2 ≈ **10 g**
- Habanero (log +1.6): 150 ÷ 10^1.6 ≈ **4 g**
- Poblano (log -0.5): 150 ÷ 10^-0.5 ≈ **475 g**

## Practical Notes

- **Poblano → Serrano** is the workhorse range for chili. These peppers contribute both flavor and heat as structural ingredients.
- **Above serrano** (Thai, habanero, scotch bonnet), you're adding small amounts for heat, not bulk. Treat them as a seasoning, not an ingredient.
- **Below poblano** (bell, shishito, banana), you're adding vegetable bulk and flavor with negligible heat.
- **Mixing pepper types** adds complexity. A few jalapeños for body + a bird's eye or two for sharp heat is a classic approach.
- Individual peppers vary. Jalapeños alone can range from 2,000 to 8,000 SHU. Taste a small piece before committing.
- **Seeds and ribs** contain the highest capsaicin concentration. Removing them reduces heat by roughly 50%.
