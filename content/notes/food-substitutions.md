_Sometimes, you don't have a particular ingredient, or it's hard to find. That's okay! Often, there are rough and ready substitutes!_
# Close substitutions
- vino seco for shaoxing wine

# Substitutions with adjustment
- buttermilk for full fat greek yogurt: difference in thickness (use water to let down yogurt)
- [[tomato-paste]] for pepper paste: pepper paste is slightly less sweet and slightly spicy. Honest preference! Use sugar to adjust if needed.

# Not comparable
- fresh garlic and powdered garlic: different flavor profiles. honestly, use both
