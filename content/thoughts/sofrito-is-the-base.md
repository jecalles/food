# Sofrito is the base
Across cultures, many dishes start by making something like a [[sofrito]] (i.e., aromatics sauteed in oil). Sofrito like this can be used as the base for simmered, braised, and stewed dishes. 
### Key ingredients
Always with the aromatics: onions, green onions, scallions, shallots, etc. Garlic and ginger are friends here too.

Often, a paste (e.g., tomato, curry, gochujang, miso, etc.) goes in right at the end and is fried up to give it some life. This paste is dependent on how thick the resulting dish will be. Roux is often used here as well.

The deglazing liquid is often a key ingredient as well. Deglaze with a liquid that is in keeping with the style you are attempting to emulate (e.g., red wine for french food, vino seco for Cuban, etc.).

### Examples
- Mirepoix: onion, carrot, celery
- "Holy Trinity" (Creole): onion, celery, bell pepper, (tomato paste?, roux)
- Cuban: onion, garlic, bell pepper, (tomato paste?)
