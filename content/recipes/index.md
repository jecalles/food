Most of what's here falls into two categories: **foundations** — building blocks that show up everywhere — and **templates** — improvisational frameworks for a type of dish rather than a fixed formula.

The templates are the more interesting half. [[improvised-braise|Improvised braise]], [[improvised-chili|improvised chili]], and [[improvised-lasagna|improvised lasagna]] are less "follow this" and more "here's how to think about it." Once the template clicks, the specific recipe becomes a guided riff.

## Foundations

Things that make other things. Start here if you're learning.

- [[sofrito]] — the base of most of my cooking; shows up across cuisines under different names
- [[mirepoix]] — French aromatic base; compare to sofrito
- [[roux]] → [[béchamel]] → [[velouté]] → [[mornay-(cheese-sauce)|Mornay]] — the French mother sauce chain
- [[tempering-oil]] — the Indian analog: bloom spices in hot oil first, build from there
- [[beef-stock]], [[chicken-stock]], [[pork-stock]] — make in bulk, freeze

## Templates

Recipes meant to be adapted, not followed exactly.

- [[improvised-braise]] — the template for all braises; works with any tough, collagen-rich meat
- [[improvised-chili]] — more of a philosophy than a recipe
- [[improvised-lasagna]] — adaptable layered pasta bake
- [[pantry-meal]] — what to make when you haven't planned
