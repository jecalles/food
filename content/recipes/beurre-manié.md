_Emulsification agent made from kneading one part flour and one part butter. Used to finish soups and sauces to thicken and make glossy . See also [[roux]]_

# Ingredients
- [ ] flour (1 part)
- [ ] butter (1 part)

# Instructions
1. Fold butter and flour together and knead until fully combined.
2. Add beurre manié off heat to sauce or soup and stir until combine. Cook out the taste of flour on low heat with stirring.
