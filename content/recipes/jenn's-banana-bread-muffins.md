# Ingredients
For one batch of 12 muffins.

_Dry_
- [ ] all purpose flour (190 g, 1.5 cup)
- [ ] granulated sugar (150 g, 3/4 cup)
- [ ] baking soda (1 tsp)
- [ ] salt (1 tsp)

_Wet_
- [ ] old bananas (2 large)
- [ ] vegetable oil (120 mL, 1/2 cup)
- [ ] vanilla (1 tsp)
- [ ] eggs (1 large)

_Optional_
- [ ] chocolate chips - based on vibes
- [ ] chopped walnuts - based on vibes

## Equipment
- [ ] oven (350F)
- [ ] scale
- [ ] measuring spoons and cups
- [ ] large mixing bowl and whisk
- [ ] small mixing bowl and fork
- [ ] muffin tin
- [ ] cupcake skirts

# Instructions
1. Weigh dry ingredients into a large mixing bowl and mix (whisk).
2. Mash bananas in a small mixing bowl.
3. Add wet ingredients to bananas in small mixing bowl and mix.
4. Folded wet ingredients into dry ingredients until just combined. DONT overwork!
5. (Optional) Add additives (chocolate chips, crushed nuts, etc.) and mix in thoroughly.
6. With a kitchen spoon or ice cream scoop, portion batter into cupcake skirts in muffin tin. Top muffins with some additives
7. Top muffins with a vibey amount of additives (~3).
8. Bake at 350F until done (~17 minutes). Test doneness by poking muffin top: should feel _just_ dry (not springy).

# Notes
- Muffins get a _slight_ crisp at the doneness level in the center that I like (closer to 20 min). I wonder if increasing the temperature (e.g., 370F / 15 min) would be better? On the other hand, crisp is not universally popular
- If using chocolate chips, the last few muffins tend to get fewer than the first few. Mix thoroughly OR just add a few more chocolate chips to the latter ones.
- "The vegetable oil is the star of this recipe, don't skimp!" 
- "The ingredients tell the story... there's no complicated technique" - Jenn(ifer)
