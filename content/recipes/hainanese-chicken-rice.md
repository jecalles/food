_[[Nad]] turned me onto chicken rice and I've been obsessed ever since. High level, you poach a chicken, then use the poaching liquid to cook rice. Served typically with dipping sauces and a simple cucumber salad._
Status: #in-practice 
Tags: [[meal-prep]]

# Ingredients

_Essential_
- [ ] Chicken (4.5 lb)
- [ ] Jasmine rice
- [ ] Garlic (one head, divided and minced)
- [ ] Ginger (not enough! small knob minced)
- [ ] Green onion (small bunch, sliced thin, whites and greens separated)
- [ ] Shallot (1x, not typical)
- [ ] vegetable oil
- [ ] stock or water
- [ ] salt

_Recommended_
- [ ] cucumber

## Equipment
- medium bowl + small plate
- ice bath
- medium pot (for poaching chicken)
- dutch oven (for rice)
- sauce pan
- sieve


# Instructions
1. Measure out poaching liquid: put chicken in poaching pot and add water until just submerged. Pull out chicken and reserve for later.
2. Bring poaching liquid to a boil. Add salt, garlic and ginger (roughly crushed or chopped).
3. Reduce heat. Gently dunk chicken, allowing water to enter and drain the main cavity. Submerge chicken and allow to come to a simmer. Pull chicken back out and allow to drain. Return to pot and bring to simmer.
4. Simmer covered for 30-35 minutes. Kill heat and let sit for 20 min.
5. (Meanwhile) Prep garlic, ginger, green onion, and shallots.
6. Pull chicken and immediate dunk in ice bath. Remove some chicken skin and fat for rendering. 
7. In a dutch oven, render chicken fat. Fry shallots and some of the garlic, ginger, and green onion. Add rice to dutch oven and toast.
8. Once fragrant, add poaching liquid. Bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer, and cook until done.
9. (Meanwhile) heat neutral oil in a saucepan. Pour over remaining garlic, ginger, and green onions to infuse.
10. Once rice is done, assemble! Fluff rice with a fork. Butcher chicken and cleave across the bones. Serve rice and chicken with sauce and optional garnishes.
