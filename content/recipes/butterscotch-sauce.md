_A delicious and sweet sauce that goes well atop baked goods. Essentially, you make a [[toffee]] and then let it down with cream._
Status: #needs-practice 
Tags: [[baking]] [[sauce]] [[caramel]]

# Ingredients
- [ ] butter (85 g, 6 tbsp)
- [ ] (brown) sugar (150 g, 3/4 cup, packed)
- [ ] heavy cream (120 mL, 1/2 cup)
- [ ] vanilla extract (10 mL, 2 tsp)
- [ ] pinch of salt

## Equipment
- sauce pan, spatula, and stove
- mixing bowl

# Instructions
1. Melt butter in saucepan over medium heat until foaming, smelling nutty, and solids are just beginning to brown. (3 min)
2. Add sugar and stir constantly until combined and the mixture begins bubbling steadily. (2 min)
3. Off the heat and stirring constantly, add in cream. Continue stirring until smooth.
4. Off the heat, add vanilla and salt.
5. Allow sauce to thicken and cool. (5 min)

# Notes
- Substituting white sugar for brown sugar gets rid of the nice toffee notes, but does let the vanilla shine.
- Hardens up when refrigerated to a soft, chewy texture.
