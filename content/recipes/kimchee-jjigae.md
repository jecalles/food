_[[korean]] kimchee stew with silken tofu._

Tags: [[cooking]]

# Ingredients

_Essential_
- [ ] kimchi (1 lb; 0.5 kg)
- [ ] silken tofu, cubed (1 package; how much?)
- [ ] green onion (3x)
- [ ] onion, sliced (1x medium)
- [ ] salt (1 tsp)
- [ ] sugar (2 tsp)
- [ ] gochugaru (2 tsp?)
- [ ] gochujang (1 tbsp?)
- [ ] toasted sesame oil (1 tsp)
- [ ] stock (2 cup; 500 mL)

_anchovy stock_
- [ ] dried anchovies (7x large)
- [ ] dried kelp (4x 5")
- [ ] green onion root (4x)
- [ ] Daikon radish, thin sliced (1/3 cup)
- [ ] water (4 cup)

_Optional_
- [ ] rice

# Equipment
- Stock pot

# Instructions
1. Make anchovy stock
	1. Put dried anchovies, daikon, green onion roots, and dried kelp in pot.
	2. Add water, bring to boil and boil for 20 minutes over medium high heat.
	3. Simmer on low heat for 5 minutes. Strain
2. Make kimchi stew
	1. Add kimchi (with brine) to stock pot.
	2. Slice 2x green onion on a bias and add to pot.
	3. Add salt, sugar, goghugaru, gochujang, and broth. Drizzle sesame oil over top. Do NOT stir in seasonings yet (I don't know why).
	4. Bring to boil and reduce to simmer. Cover and simmer for 10 min.
	5. Stir in seasonings to combine.
	6. Lay in silken tofu cubes, cover and simmer for another 10 to 15 minutes.
3. Garnish and serve. 
	1. Chop 1x green onion and decorate stew. 
	2. Serve atop rice.
