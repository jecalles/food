# Instructions
1. Polenta
	1. Salt cooking liquid and bring to a boil.
	2. Reduce to a simmer, and pour in dry polenta SLOWLY while whisking constantly. Avoid lumps.
	3. Cook on low until done to your liking (30 - 45 min). Stir every few minutes to avoid scorching.
