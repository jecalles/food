# Ingredients
2 water : 1 chicken bones (on stovetop)
1.5 water : 1 chicken bones (in pressure cooker)

_Essential_
- [ ] chicken bones (1000 g)
- [ ] water (2L stovetop, 1.5 L pressure cooker)

_Recommended_
- [ ] onion (1 large, quartered)
- [ ] garlic (6 cloves, peeled and smashed)
- [ ] black peppercorns (1 tsp, whole)

## Equipment
- [[dutch-oven]] and stovetop XOR [[pressure-cooker]]

# Instructions
1. Add bones, aromatics, and water to vessel (dutch oven or pressure cooker)
2. Cook low and slow
	- If stovetop: bring to boil, reduce to simmer, and leave covered for (2-6) hrs.
	- If pressure cooker: high pressure for 45 min, natural release.
3. Strain. Store at 4C for up to a week and frozen for longer.
