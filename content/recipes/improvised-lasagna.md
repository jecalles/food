_Sometimes you just want to make a lot of food for a lot of people and not have to faff around with a recipe. This is the meal for you. Spinach adds some bulk without heaviness and extends fresh basil if you want to do a layer of greens. Some recipes alternate red and white sauce._

# Ingredients
For 2x Three Layer Lasagnas (9"x13")
- [ ] 2x box of lasagna noodles (~2 lb)
- [ ] 4 jar tomato sauce (~2L)
- [ ] ground beef (1 lb, 454g; partially thawed)
- [ ] italian sausage (~150 g)
- [ ] 1x large onion, diced
- [ ] 1x head of garlic, minced
- [ ] 2x tub ricotta cheese (30 oz ~ 900 mL)
- [ ] mozzarella cheese (2 lb, 900g, shredded)
- [ ] pecorino romano (250 g, grated)
- [ ] basil (one package, 4 oz?)
- [ ] spinach (one whole bag tho)
- [ ] Bottle of chianti (deglazing, mostly for the chef)
- [ ] decent balsamic vinegar (splash)
- [ ] dried Italian herb blend
- [ ] Salt (to taste)

# Method 
*Make sauce*
1. Preheat dutch oven on medium flame.
2. Fry up italian sausage to render fat.
3. Add partially thawed ground beef. Realize it's frozen. Cover dutch oven with lid and use steam to defrost. Uncover and evaporate all the water until it can fry again.
4. Saute onion and garlic in rendered fat.
5. Deglaze with red wine.
6. Add sauce and dried herb. Bring to a simmer. 

*Assemble Lasagna*: 
For each layer of the lasagna (we did three layers)
1. Red sauce layer
2. Noodle layer
3. Cheese layer: dollop of ricotta, spread. Add moderate covering of shredded mozzarella and grated pecorino.
4. Green Layer: modest layer of basil, and enough spinach to fully cover the cheese layer

*Bake Lasagna* 
1. Bake at 400F covered in aluminum foil for 40 minutes.
2. Uncover and bake for another 10 minutes.
3. Check for doneness (cheese is golden, knife pierces cleanly), else return.
# Notes for next time
- Could've used more red sauce even still! Maybe well thinned out with some more wine, or some stock.
- Didn't use dried herbs, but basil and garlic gave a fresh flavor that balanced against the fat from the meats. Red pepper or calabrian chili would be great.
- Had to get creative with laying out sheets of lasagna noodles. I liked rotating layers, such that noodles are at 90 degree angles to each other.
- For the last layer of the lasagna, we added a little bit of spinach primarily for garnish
