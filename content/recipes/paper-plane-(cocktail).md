In an ice shaker, combine equal parts (30 mL) bourbon, Aperol, Amaro, and lemon juice. Serve chilled, straight up, with a lemon twist.
# Ingredients
- [ ] bourbon (30 mL)
- [ ] Aperol (30 mL)
- [ ] Amaro (30 mL)
- [ ] lemon juice, fresh (30 mL)
- [ ] ice
## Equipment
- Coupe glass
- Shaker
# Instructions
1. Combine liquids in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake or stir until cold, then strain into coupe glasses.
3. Peel lemon, express oils over the cocktail, twist, and present on the rim of the glass.
