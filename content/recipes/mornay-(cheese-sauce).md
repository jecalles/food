_A child of the French Mother sauce [[béchamel]]. Classic Mornay sauce is 2:1 w/w béchamel to cheese, or 1 flour:1 butter:20 milk:11 cheese. Can use a range of ratios to adjust consistency, typically between 4:1 and 1:1 w/w._
Status: #needs-practice 
Tags: [[sauce]] 


| Béchamel : Cheese (w/w) | Béchamel : Cheese (v/v) | Consistency | Application |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| 4:1 | ~6:1 | Thin, pourable | Light cheese sauce for pasta, vegetables |
| 3:1 | ~4.5:1 | Medium, coats a spoon | Macaroni and cheese sauce, nacho drizzle |
| 2:1 | ~3:1 | Thick but still pourable | Classic Mornay, dipping sauce, queso-style dip |
| 3:2 | ~2:1 | Thick, slow pour | Hearty chip dip, casserole binder |
| 1:1 | ~1.5:1 | Very thick, almost paste-like | Stuffing for croquettes, filling for fried items |

# Ingredients
- [ ] butter (1 part)
- [ ] flour (1 part)
- [ ] milk (20 parts)
- [ ] cheese (11 parts)

# Instructions
1. Make a [[roux]].
2. Make a [[béchamel]].
3. Take béchamel off the heat. Slowly fold in cheese one palm at a time, stirring until incorporated. 

# Notes
- Add the cheese on heat and it might get grainy.
