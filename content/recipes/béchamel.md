_A white sauce built up from one part [[roux]] (butter) and ten parts whole milk (1:1:20 butter, flour, and milk). Useful for making cheese sauces._

# Ingredients

_Essential_
- [ ] butter (1 part)
- [ ] flour (1 part)
- [ ] milk (20 parts)
- [ ] salt (to taste)

_Optional_
- [ ] onion (peeled and halved)
- [ ] pepper (to taste)

# Instructions
1. Make a white [[roux]].
	1. Melt butter over medium low heat.
	2. Whisk in flour until combined.
	3. Stir constantly over medium heat until flour is _just_ cooked out.
2. Prepare milk. 
	1. Peel and halve onion.
	2. In a separate saucepan, heat milk to a simmer.
	3. Add onion and bay leaf. Stir over medium heat to extract flavor.
	4. Remove onion and bayleaf OR strain off milk.
3. Gradually whisk heated milk into roux until all is incorporated.
4. Add salt and pepper to taste.
5. Stir over medium heat until thickened and no lumps remain (about 15 min).
