_A pastry crust. Approximately 1 part flour : 1 part butter : 0.5 part sugar._
Status: #needs-practice 
Tags: [[baking]] [[ratios]]

# Ingredients
_dry_
- [ ] all purpose flour (260g, 2 c)
- [ ] granulated sugar (100 g, 1/2 c)
- [ ] salt (1/4 tsp)
_wet_
- [ ] unsalted butter, room temp (225 g, 8 oz)
# Equipment
- Oven (350 F, 175C)
- Stand mixer with paddle attachment
- 9"x13" baking tray and parchment paper

# Instructions
1. In a stand mixer, combine dry ingredients on low speed: flour, sugar, salt. 
2. Dice butter. Mix in on low speed until dough forms a ball (approx. 2 min).
3. Spread dough evenly into baking tray lined with parchment paper.
4. Bake (350F/175C) until golden.
