# Ingredients
1 parts stale bread : 3 parts custard. For less absorbant breads you may need more custard.

_Essential_
- [ ] stale bread (500 g)
- [ ] whole milk (750 mL)
- [ ] heavy cream (250 mL)
- [ ] 4 large eggs (200 g)
- [ ] granulated sugar (100 g, 1/2 cup)
- [ ] vanilla extract (2 tsp)
- [ ] cinnamon powder (2 tsp)
- [ ] pinch of salt

_Recommended_: [[butterscotch sauce]]
- [ ] butter (85 g, 6 tbsp)
- [ ] sugar (150 g, 3/4 cup, packed)
- [ ] heavy cream (120 mL, 1/2 cup)
- [ ] vanilla extract (10 mL, 2 tsp)
- [ ] pinch of salt

## Equipment
- Oven (325F / 165C)
- 9"x13" baking tray

# Instructions
1. Prepare Bread: Cut or tear stale bread into smaller pieces or chunks. Spread out in an even layer the baking tin.
2. Make Custard: Whisk eggs in a mixing bowl. Add milk, cream, sugar, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt. Whisk thoroughly until combined. 
3. Soak bread: pour custard over bread layer until just covering. Cover and and let soak fully in the refrigerator (up to overnight).
4. Bake at 325F uncovered for 40-50 minutes and check for doneness. Center should slightly jiggle.
5. (Meanwhile) make [[butterscotch sauce]]
6. Remove from oven and let cool and set for 15 minutes.
