_French version of [[sofrito]]. 2 onion : 1 carrot : 1 celery. This often turns out to be 1x onion, 1x carrot, 1x celery. Fun!_
Status: #in-practice 
Tags: [[cooking-technique]]

# Ingredients

_Essential_
- [ ] onion, diced
- [ ] carrot, diced
- [ ] celery, diced
- [ ] oil (e.g., olive, neutral, butter)
- [ ] salt

_Basically Essential_
- [ ] garlic, minced
- [ ] black pepper

_Recommended_
- [ ] herbs (basically any)

# Equipment
- pan and stovetop
- knife and cutting board

# Instructions
1. Dice onion, carrot, and celery. Mince garlic.
2. In a pan over medium heat, saute onion with oil until just translucent and fragrant. Season with salt.
3. Add carrots, stir, season with salt, and saute until just softened.
4. Add celery, stir, season with salt, and saute until just softened.
5. Add garlic, stir, and saute until fragrant
