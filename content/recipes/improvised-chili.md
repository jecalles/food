# Ingredients

NOTE: all macro ingredients are scaled to the amount of beans (weight).

E.g., for 1000 g [[beans]], cooked (approx. 4 cans, drained and rinsed / 1 lb dry beans cooked to doneness).

(0 - 1) meat : beans (e.g., 2 lb lean ground beef, none if vegan)
(0.7 - 1) tomato : beans (e.g., 750 g = 150 g tomato paste + 600 g tomato sauce)
0.5 aromatics : beans (e.g., 1x large onion, 2x bell pepper, 1x small head of garlic)
(0.1 - 0.3) spices : beans (e.g., seasoning to taste throughout with chili powder -> 200g )
(enough) [[chili-peppers]] : beans (e.g., 2x jalapeño is mild, 2x birds eye chilis may be medium?)
(0.25 - 1) liquid (as needed): beans (e.g., 250 mL stock; in reality, enough to cover)


_Essential_
- [ ] beans (1000 g, cooked and drained)
- [ ] tomato (810 g)
	- [ ] tomato sauce (680 g)
	- [ ] tomato paste (130 g)
- [ ] aromatics (500 g, diced)
	- [ ] alium: onion, shallot, green onion, etc. (diced)
	- [ ] garlic (minced)
	- [ ] bell peppers (diced)
	- [ ] chili peppers (fresh, diced with seeds)
- [ ] spices (100 g, ish, season throughout with more than you think)
- [ ] cooking liquid (300 g, as needed)
- [ ] oil or fat (e.g., olive oil, bacon grease, etc.) 
- [ ] salt to taste


_Optional_
- [ ] ground meat (500 g, uncooked)

## Equipment
- knife and cutting board
- dutch oven and stove
- (if dry beans) [[pressure-cooker]] (see [[pressure-cook-beans]] for details)

# Instructions
1. In a dutch oven over medium heat, fry ground meat. Season to taste (overseason). Remove and reserve.
2. In the same dutch oven, make a [[sofrito]] from the aromatics, chilis and tomatoes. Add spices and seasoning.
3. Add cooked beans to dutch oven and bring to temperature.
4. Add cooking liquid, bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer and cover to let cook until done, at least 45 min. Longer the better (target +2 hr).
5. Adjust seasoning.

# Notes
- It's extremely difficult, if not impossible, to overcook chili. The longer the slow simmer, the better.
- Use more spice than you think you need. This is the base heat.
- Fresh chili are the fresh heat.
